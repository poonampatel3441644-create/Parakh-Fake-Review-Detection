How Parakh works

Three steps, and every answer comes with its evidence.

1

Paste a review

Copy any product review into the box, or start with one of the examples.

2

Two models read it

One model judges whether the writing looks genuine or machine-written. The other reads whether the review is positive, neutral or negative.

3

See the evidence

You get a verdict, a gauge, and the words that pushed the result each way, so the answer is never a black box.

Under the hood

A student machine-learning project, built from data to web app.

40,420reviews used for training and testing
90.3%accuracy for the authenticity model on held-out test reviews
2 modelsauthenticity and sentiment, both TF-IDF with Logistic Regression
DjangoPython web app with scikit-learn, saved models and SQLite

Parakh was trained on one public dataset that compares original reviews with computer-generated ones. Results on other websites may differ, sentiment labels come from star ratings, and every verdict is an estimate, not proof.

Parakh is a student project for learning and demonstration.
